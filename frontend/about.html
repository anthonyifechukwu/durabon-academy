The Story Behind DURABON

Character First, Always

DURABON Academy was founded in Uromi, Edo State, on the belief that a great school does more than teach subjects — it shapes the person a child is becoming. Every classroom, teacher and activity is built around that idea.

Our staff track each student's progress closely, pairing rigorous academics with mentorship, discipline and genuine care, so every child leaves DURABON ready for the next stage of life.
