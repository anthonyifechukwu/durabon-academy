Welcome to DURABON ACADEMY

Where Knowledge Meets Character

At DURABON Academy, we are committed to providing a supportive and inspiring learning environment where every student can discover their potential, develop strong character, and build the skills needed for a successful future.

Through quality education, dedicated teachers, and a strong focus on both academic and personal development, we prepare our students to become confident, responsible, and lifelong learners.

ABOUT DURABON

Rest not a moment from learning.

Durabon Academy is a private school located in Uromi, Edo State. The school was founded in 2016 to provide excellent education and character development for students.

Our Mission

To provide excellent education and character development for students.

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution that nurtures responsible and successful individuals.

Our Values

We value integrity, respect, responsibility, and a commitment to lifelong learning.
